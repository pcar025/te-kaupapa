# Kaitiakitanga PHQ-9 pilot addendum — 2026-09-22

Supporting practice source: **TE WAHAROA O TE KAUPAPA – Documentation, Safety & Evidencing Standard**, Version 1 (August 2026), Anneke Hepi – NHC Te Kaupapa Practice Lead, status Draft.

This operator-approved pilot addendum authorises only ordinary Kaitiakitanga conversational guidance. It preserves the approved opening question exactly:

> Thinking about the person’s current situation, what risks, pressures or vulnerabilities did you notice, what strengths or supports may help protect them, and what still needs to be understood?

The conversation may explore current and changing risk, protective factors, and the response already considered or taken. It may then ask whether the Kaimahi judged PHQ-9 indicated. Only when the answer is yes may it ask whether PHQ-9 was completed and, only when completed, the Kaimahi-reported total score. Missing, declined, or not-yet-known information remains unknown; the conversation must not infer it.

The conversational AI must not administer or score PHQ-9 items, diagnose, select concern levels, decide safety or escalation, or create actions, referrals, observations, or notifications.

The addendum is not approval of the wider draft document as executable safety policy. It adds no formal safety rules. Only explicit Kaimahi confirmation may create authoritative PHQ-9 state; the existing backend rule alone derives score >=12 supervisor escalation required.
